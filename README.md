# Online Retail Big Data Analytics Project

## 📊 Overview
This project demonstrates end-to-end data analytics using Python and Power BI on an online retail dataset.

- Python (Pandas)
- Power BI
- Excel
- Data Visualization

## 📂 Project Workflow
1. Raw data collection
2. Data cleaning using Python
3. Feature engineering (TotalSales)
4. Data visualization using Power BI
5. Business insights generation

📈 BUSINESS INSIGHTS 
💰 1. Revenue & Sales Insights
United Kingdom is the dominant revenue contributor, accounting for the majority of total sales.
Sales show strong seasonal patterns, with peak demand observed during end-of-year (December) period.
A small proportion of transactions generate a large share of total revenue, indicating a Pareto distribution (80/20 rule).
📦 2. Product Insights
A limited number of products contribute to the majority of sales revenue.
High-demand products are consistently purchased across multiple time periods, indicating stable customer preferences.
Some products show high quantity sold but lower revenue, indicating low-priced bulk items.
👥 3. Customer Insights
A small group of high-value customers contribute significantly to total revenue.
Customer frequency analysis shows repeat customers are responsible for a major portion of sales.
Customer segmentation can be categorized into:
High-value loyal customers
Regular buyers
Occasional customers
🌍 4. Country-Level Insights
UK dominates the sales distribution, showing strong domestic market dependency.
Other countries such as Germany, France, Netherlands, and EIRE show growth potential for expansion.
International markets collectively contribute a smaller but strategic portion of revenue.
📊 5. Time Series Insights
Sales trend analysis shows consistent growth with seasonal spikes.
Monthly analysis reveals peak sales during festive and holiday seasons.
Quantity trends align closely with revenue trends, confirming demand-driven sales patterns.
🧾 6. Invoice-Level Insights
Most invoices contain multiple product purchases, indicating bulk buying behavior.
High-value invoices contribute disproportionately to total revenue.
Invoice-level analysis helps identify high-value transactions and customer spending behavior.
🚀 7. Strategic Business Insights
Over-reliance on a single country (UK) presents business risk in diversification.
Product portfolio is concentrated, suggesting opportunity for product expansion strategy.
Customer segmentation can be used for targeted marketing and loyalty programs.
Data-driven decision making improves inventory planning and reduces stock inefficiencies.

FINAL SUMMARY

This analysis demonstrates that retail performance is driven by a combination of:

Seasonal demand
High-value customers
Key product categories

Using Python and Power BI, raw transactional data has been transformed into actionable business intelligence that supports strategic decision-making, market expansion, and customer retention strategies.
